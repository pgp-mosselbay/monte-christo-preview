The Village

Secure living, surrounded by indigenous nature and birdlife

Monte Christo Retirement Village forms part of the expansive 54-hectare Monte Christo Eco Estate, just 15 km from Mossel Bay along the western side of the N2.

Set on an elevated ridge, the village enjoys sweeping views across Hartenbos, Mossel Bay, and the majestic Outeniqua Mountains. Surrounded by indigenous vegetation and alive with abundant birdlife, the Eco Estate offers a tranquil, nature-rich environment where retirement living feels both secure and inspiring.

Village life

A clubhouse, a library and good company

Monte Christo Retirement Village enhances daily living with a wealth of amenities, including a welcoming clubhouse with catering services, a ladies’ bar, and a well-stocked library.

A full social calendar

Something to look forward to every week

Residents and guests come together for dances, bingo evenings, wine tastings, high teas and the Chatterbox ladies’ mornings, with refreshments from BiBi’s Kitchen in the clubhouse.

Health care

Care on site, with comfort, dignity and confidence

To provide residents with complete peace of mind, Monte Christo Retirement Village offers assisted living, home care and frail care, together with an on-site clinic. Its highly trained staff are dedicated to ensuring that every resident enjoys their golden years with comfort, dignity, and confidence.

Home care packages range from half-day support to round-the-clock care, with regular visits by a Registered Nurse. For those who need full-time care, the frail care facility offers private and shared en-suite rooms, with meals, laundry and 24-hour care included.

Monte Christo Health Care

Here for you, 24 hours a day, seven days a week

Sister Harriet-Ann Rabie leads the care team, and two caregivers are on duty through the night. Since 1 September 2026 the facility has been owned by Inelia Loots, whose office is next to the Treatment Room.

  • Two caregivers on duty every night
  • Regular blood pressure checks in the Treatment Room, included in the Basic Health Care levy
  • Emergency trolley that can be taken to a resident’s home
  • Personal emergency (PEMS) button system, tested regularly
  • Wheelchairs, walkers and crutches available for hire
  • Night-time caregiver visits by arrangement

Levels of care

Monday to Friday, by appointment. Free of charge for village residents.

Good to know

Before you move in

  • Health assessment before you buyEvery new resident completes a DQ98 health assessment with Monte Christo Care Facility (R250) and provides a recommendation from their GP before signing a sale or lease agreement.
  • Care by our in-house teamTo ensure safety and continuity, all care in the village is provided by the in-house team. Outside care agencies do not operate on site, although family members and registered specialists are welcome.
  • Advanced cognitive conditionsPlease note that the care facility does not cater for advanced cognitive conditions.
